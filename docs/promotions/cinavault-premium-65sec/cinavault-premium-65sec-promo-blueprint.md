# CinaVault Premium — 65-Second Hollywood Promo Blueprint

## Creative direction

This is a **16:9 cinematic technology commercial** with a glossy, forward-looking Hollywood finish. The visual language combines deep graphite space, CinaVault’s authentic orange-to-blue mountain/media artwork, cyan holographic overlays, volumetric light, lens flares, metallic 3D frame geometry, and elegant camera movement. The opening and closing use a deliberate fade from and to black. The mood is ambitious, premium, and energetic without becoming noisy.

The audience is the same tech-savvy young-adult audience used for the CinaCLAW promo. The voice is English, slow, deep, resonant, and male with a subtle, tasteful Southern drawl. The CTA is to visit the CinaVault Premium GitHub repository: `github.com/johngraven75/CinaVault-Premium`.

The repository checkout contains authentic branding assets but no complete UI screen-capture library. Therefore, the promo uses the real CinaVault logo/banner artwork for brand shots and presents feature moments as clearly stylized **feature visualizations**, not fabricated screenshots. The claims come from the repository’s build summaries and PGMA notes.

## Voiceover script

> Meet CinaVault Premium. [short pause] Your media library, upgraded into a smarter, cleaner, more cinematic experience. Scan your collection, enrich titles and artwork, and keep the details close to the files they belong to. Posters, sidecars, ratings, genres, and remote artwork—organized with a workflow built for real libraries. Play what you want, when you want, with click-to-play reliability and dependable Windows tools for repair and inspection. Bring remote access, secure authentication, library enrichment, pagination, and a full-library visual refresh into one focused app. And with PGMA-powered metadata bridging, local artwork and NFO data can flow back into your library database. CinaVault Premium. Make your collection feel like a world of its own. Explore the latest build at GitHub, and bring your library into focus.

Approximate length: 126 words. At the documented English narration rate of roughly 2.1 words per second, the read lands near 60 seconds; cinematic pauses and title-card holds extend the finished spot to 65 seconds.

## Production rules

The authentic logo and banner artwork must not be redrawn, recolored, or covered by animated text. Holographic panels are feature visualizations and must not be presented as literal screenshots. The spot may claim documented Windows functionality and the documented metadata/artwork bridge, but it must not claim a mobile production release, permanent provider availability, or a specific third-party service unless a current release note verifies it.

## Required deliverables

The final package should contain the 65-second 16:9 MP4, the English male voiceover WAV, this shot blueprint, the production script, a visual-provenance note, and SHA-256 checksums.
